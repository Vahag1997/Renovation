RESIDENTIAL CASE STUDY

The Monolith Residence

LOCATION

Tribeca, New York

AREA

220m² Interior

DURATION

7 Months

SCOPE

Full Renovation

Designing for silence in the heart of the city.

The Monolith Residence was born from a desire to create a sanctuary of brutalist elegance within the bustling energy of Tribeca. Our approach was reductive, stripping the space back to its architectural essentials. We replaced traditional partition walls with sculptural volumes that define function without closing off the expansive vistas of the Manhattan skyline.

RAW CONCRETE

Hand-poured concrete panels provide a rhythmic backdrop throughout the main living areas, offering a tactile, matte texture that absorbs light rather than reflecting it.

SMOKED OAK

Deeply stained smoked oak was utilized for all bespoke joinery, providing a warm, organic counterbalance to the industrial coolness of the masonry.
